## Design Context

### Users
Mail Wolf is a public SaaS for Gmail users whose inboxes have accumulated years of promotions, notifications, receipts, account alerts, and personal correspondence. They want to reclaim their mailbox without losing financial records, security events, contracts, bookings, attachments, or meaningful conversations. The primary job is to scan, understand, review, and safely clean email in reversible batches.

### Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, precise. The product should reduce anxiety and make destructive actions feel controlled and understandable. Its voice is plain-spoken and candid: it explains why messages were classified, distinguishes estimates from exact counts, and never pressures users into deleting.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined editorial utility with both light and dark themes. The interface should feel more like a well-organized household ledger than a generic analytics dashboard: warm paper-like neutrals, deep ink, a restrained green for safe actions, and an amber accent for review states. Avoid neon AI aesthetics, glassmorphism, generic card grids, and playful visuals that weaken trust.

### Design Principles
1. Make every destructive action reversible, scoped, and previewable.
2. Show representative emails and explicit exclusions before showing an approval button.
3. Prefer calm hierarchy and concise language over dense dashboard chrome.
4. Treat Gmail categories and AI classifications as evidence, never unquestionable truth.
5. Meet WCAG AA, support keyboard navigation and reduced motion, and preserve full functionality in light and dark themes.
